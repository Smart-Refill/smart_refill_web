What is Smart Refill?

Smart Refill is a network of automated refill machines for everyday household essentials such as laundry liquid, dishwash liquid, floor cleaner and handwash. You bring your own bottle or reusable container, choose the product and quantity, and pay only for what is dispensed, so you reuse packaging instead of buying a new single-use bottle each time. Each machine works as a neighbourhood refill station, placed in residential communities and other high-footfall locations in India.

Questions, answered.

What is Smart Refill?

Smart Refill is a network of automated refill machines for everyday household essentials such as laundry liquid, dishwash liquid, floor cleaner and handwash. You bring your own bottle, refill the quantity you need and pay only for what is dispensed.

What is a refill station?

A refill station is a machine where you refill your own bottle with a household liquid instead of buying a new packaged one. Smart Refill machines work this way for laundry liquid, dishwash, floor cleaner and handwash.

How does a Smart Refill machine work?

Bring your existing bottle or reusable container, choose your product and quantity at the machine, then pay for the quantity dispensed. There is no new packaging to buy and no complicated process.

Can I refill laundry detergent, dishwash and handwash?

Yes. Smart Refill machines are designed for liquid laundry detergent, dishwash liquid, floor cleaner, handwash and other household essentials. Availability varies by machine and location.

Do I need to buy a special bottle?

No. You can bring a bottle or reusable container you already have.

How is refill pricing decided?

Pricing is based on the quantity you dispense, so you pay for what you need instead of a fixed pack size.

How can I reduce plastic waste at home?

One simple step is to reuse the bottles you already have. Refilling household liquids such as laundry liquid, dishwash and handwash in your own container means fewer single-use bottles to throw away.

Does refilling reduce plastic packaging?

Refilling lets you reuse a container you already own instead of buying a new single-use bottle each time, which means less single-use packaging. Smart Refill does not claim that any product is organic, chemical-free or 100% environmentally safe.

Is Smart Refill zero waste or plastic free?

Not on its own. Smart Refill is one practical step towards zero-waste and plastic-free habits, using reusable packaging in place of repeated single-use bottles. We publish impact figures only when they are verified.

Where can I find a refill station near me?

Machines are placed in residential communities and high-footfall locations. To ask about a station near you, use the enquiry form on this page, message us on WhatsApp or call +91 87626 41691.

Can my apartment, housing society or RWA get a refill machine?

Yes, you can enquire. Resident welfare associations, housing societies, facility managers, property developers, retail spaces, institutions and brand partners can reach us through the Partner With Us form, WhatsApp or phone.

What is reusable packaging?

Reusable packaging is a container that is used many times instead of being thrown away after one use. With Smart Refill, your own bottle becomes the reusable packaging: you refill it instead of replacing it.
