1. Who we are and where this applies

This Privacy Policy explains how Smart Refill (“we”, “us”) collects, uses, shares and protects your personal data when you use the Smart Refill mobile app, our refill machines, this website, or contact us by phone or WhatsApp. Smart Refill is operated by [legal name of company], with its registered office at [registered address].

Our services are intended for use in India only, and this policy is written under the laws of India, including the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the rules under it, and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) as its provisions come into force.

2. What we collect

When you register in the app

  • Your name
  • Your mobile number, which we verify with a one-time password (OTP) and use to identify your account

We ask only for what we need to create and run your account.

When you use the service

  • If the app lets you refill, pay or view past refills, the related details, such as the machine used, product, quantity, time and payment status.
  • Basic technical information about your device and app (for example app version, device model and operating system) used to keep the app working and secure.
  • Messages you send to our support team, including on WhatsApp or by phone.

When you fill in the enquiry form on our website

  • Name, phone number, email, apartment or community name, city and type of enquiry.

3. Why we use your data

  • To create your account, verify your mobile number and keep your account secure.
  • To provide the refill service and show you your activity in the app.
  • To respond to your questions, enquiries and requests, including partnership and community enquiries.
  • To send service messages such as OTPs, receipts and important updates about your account or the service.
  • To improve the app and machines, fix problems and prevent misuse or fraud.
  • To meet legal and regulatory obligations.

We send promotional messages only if you have agreed to receive them, and you can opt out at any time.

4. Consent

We process your personal data based on your consent, which you give when you register or submit a form, and for certain uses permitted by law, such as meeting legal obligations. You can withdraw your consent at any time by contacting us (see Contact below). Withdrawing consent may mean we can no longer provide the app account or service that relies on it. Withdrawal does not affect processing done before you withdrew.

5. Who we share data with

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only as needed with:

  • Service providers who help us run the service under our instructions, such as SMS or OTP delivery, cloud hosting, payment processing, customer support and communication tools.
  • Brand and operating partners, only where it is needed to deliver or support the service, and not for their own marketing without your consent.
  • Authorities, where required by law or to protect our rights, users or the public.
  • A buyer or successor, if our business is restructured or transferred, in which case this policy continues to protect your data.

If a service provider stores or processes data outside India, we do so only as permitted by Indian law.

6. How long we keep your data

We keep your personal data for as long as your account is active and as needed for the purposes above, then delete or anonymise it unless the law requires us to keep it longer (for example for accounting, tax or dispute purposes). Enquiry form data is kept for as long as needed to handle your enquiry and follow-up.

7. How we protect your data

We use reasonable security measures, including access controls and encrypted connections, to protect your data against unauthorised access, loss or misuse. No system is completely secure, so please keep your phone and OTPs private. If a breach affecting your personal data occurs, we will notify you and the authorities as required by law.

8. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you can:

  • Ask for a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how we use it.
  • Ask us to correct or update inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Ask us to delete your data and your account.
  • Withdraw your consent.
  • Make a complaint to us and have it addressed (see Grievance Officer below).
  • Nominate another person to exercise your rights if you die or become unable to do so.

To use any of these rights, contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity, for example with an OTP to your registered number.

9. Children

Our app and services are for adults. They are not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe a child has registered, contact us and we will delete the data. Where the law allows a child to use the service, we will obtain verifiable consent from a parent or lawful guardian.

10. This website, videos and WhatsApp

This website does not currently use advertising cookies. The page loads fonts from Google Fonts and shows videos from YouTube. When you play a video or when fonts load, Google/YouTube may receive technical data such as your IP address under their own privacy policies. Enquiry form submissions pass through a form-processing service (Web3Forms), which delivers them to our email. If you contact us on WhatsApp, WhatsApp’s own privacy policy also applies to that conversation. Links to Instagram, LinkedIn and other sites are governed by those sites’ policies.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example as Indian data protection rules come into force. We will post the updated version here with a new date and, for significant changes, tell you in the app or by message.
